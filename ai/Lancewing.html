How to joust

Tap flap to climb. The rider whose lance is higher on impact wins. Level lances clang and bounce apart.

Beaten rivals drop eggs. Grab them for points. Leave one too long and it hatches a knight on foot, and a riderless bird swoops in from the edge of the sky to carry him back into battle as a tougher rider. Trample him before it arrives.

Stay off the lava: a molten hand reaches for low fliers. Mash flap to break free.

Linger too long in a wave and a wyvern hunts you. Only a level lance straight into its open jaws brings it down.
