Lemmings (Amiga) — browser player

A player for the original Lemmings (DMA Design / Psygnosis, 1991) that runs from your own Amiga disk images. It reads the two game disks directly: their file system, DMA's packed files, the graphics, the lemming animations, the level files and the game's sound effects. No game data is included in this page and nothing is uploaded. Pick the disks once and, if you like, the page keeps a private copy in your browser.

The game rules are adapted from Lemmings.ts by Thomas Zeugner (MIT licence) and extended with steel, one‑way walls and deadly traps. All 120 one‑player levels and the 20 two‑player levels are here in the Amiga order (Fun, Tricky, Taxing, Mayhem), including the special full‑screen levels.

What you need

The two disks of Amiga Lemmings as .adf files (disk 1 is the program disk, disk 2 holds the levels), or a .zip containing them. Select both at once. Standard dumps and cracked single‑file versions both work.

Playing

Pick a skill on the panel at the bottom, then click a lemming to give it that skill. The number on each button is how many you have left. Hover any button and the status line says what it does.

− and +The release rate: how quickly lemmings drop out of the hatch (1–99). − slows it down (never below the level's starting rate), + speeds it up. Hold to change quickly.
PawsPauses the game and opens the menu.
Mushroom cloudNuke: every lemming becomes a bomber. Click it once — it flashes red and asks “click again to nuke!” — then click again within 1½ seconds.
Small mapClick or drag on it to jump to that part of the level.

Save the percentage shown before the level starts. Press F (or Y on a gamepad, or Speed in the menu) for fast forward; “>>” shows on the status line while it's on.

Music

The game's own tunes play from your disks: the original tracker songs and instruments, replayed the way the Amiga's sound chip did, with the Can‑Can, “Lemming 1–3”, Tim's tunes and the special tunes for the Beast, Awesome and Menacing levels. Turn music or sound effects on and off in the menu.

Two players

Choose 2 Player on the title screen for the 20 head‑to‑head levels. The screen splits: Blue plays the left half, Green the right, each with their own skills, panel, map and nuke. You can only give skills to your own lemmings, and every lemming that walks into your exit counts for you — whoever's lemming it is. Most lemmings home wins. Play it on a touch screen (one player each side), with two gamepads (pad 1 Blue, pad 2 Green), or with mouse/pad 1 for Blue and pad 2 for Green.

Controls

MouseClick a lemming to give it the selected skill. Click panel buttons. Move to the left or right edge to scroll, or use the wheel or right‑drag. The crosshair (white with a black outline) becomes a yellow box when it's over a lemming; over the panel and menus you get the normal mouse pointer. Hovering a skill button highlights it and shows its name; the status line shows what that lemming is doing.
Touch screen
(phone, tablet, DeX)		Tap a lemming to give it the skill (taps have a little extra reach). Drag sideways on the level to scroll. Tap or drag on the small map to jump. Tap ❚❚ (top right) for the menu.
Keyboard1–8 or F3–F10 pick a skill · F1/F2 or −/= release rate · ← → or A D scroll · Space assigns at the cursor · F11 pause · N or F12 nuke (twice) · F fast forward on/off · G smooth/pixel graphics · P / Esc menu. The mouse wheel over the panel changes skill.
GamepadLeft stick moves the cursor · A assigns · LB/RB pick a skill · LT/RT release rate · right stick or d‑pad scrolls · X pause · Y fast forward · Select nuke (twice) · Start menu. In menus: stick or d‑pad to move, A to choose, B to go back.
Meta Quest / VRChoose Play in VR / AR from the menu. The game floats in front of you; point a controller at it. Trigger clicks · thumbstick left/right scrolls · A/X next/previous skill · grip pause · B/Y menu · thumbstick click leaves VR.

Menu

Press P or Esc, the paws button on the panel, the ❚❚ button, Start, or B/Y in VR. It has Resume, Restart level, Speed (normal or fast), Music and Sound effects on/off, Graphics (Smooth rounds off the jagged pixel edges with a pixel‑art upscaler; Pixels shows the original blocky look), Sound on/off, Choose a level, Nuke, Fullscreen, Play in VR/AR, this read me, and Load other disks. Completed levels are remembered in your browser and marked green on the level chooser.
