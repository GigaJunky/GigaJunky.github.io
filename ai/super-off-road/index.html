The race

Four trucks, one stadium. Hit the jumps, dodge mud and water, and grab nitro cans and cash bags. Prize money buys tires, shocks, acceleration, top speed and more nitro between races.

Joining

On the Drivers screen, press gas on any keyboard or controller to take a seat. Click Mouse or Touch on a seat for those. Press Q / Backspace / B to leave a seat. Enter or Start begins.

Controller not responding? Options → Controller setup lets you pick its gas, brake and nitro buttons.

Esc pauses. M mutes music.
