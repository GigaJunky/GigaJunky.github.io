Flying tips. Taildraggers swing left under power: feed in right rudder (E) on the takeoff roll and ease the stick forward to lift the tail. Lift off near 110 mph, then raise the gear. On the carrier, drop the hook (H), lower full flaps, and come in slow over the stern to catch a wire.
