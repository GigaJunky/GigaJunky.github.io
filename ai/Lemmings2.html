Lemmings 2: The Tribes — Amiga disks player

Select all three of your Amiga disk images (Lemmings2_1.adf, _2, _3) or a .zip holding them. Everything runs here in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

All 12 tribes and all 51 skills are in, the tribes' own music and the game's sound effects play from your disks. Skills are recreated from how the original game behaves, so a few details may differ. The fan button (or V) turns the pointer into a fan: hold it down near floating lemmings (floaters, balloons, gliders, jet packs) to blow them away from the pointer.
Controls: click a lemming to give it the selected skill · 1–8 or the wheel over the panel picks a skill · arrows/WASD, mouse at the edges, right-drag or touch-drag scroll · Space pause · F fast · N nuke (twice) · G smooth/pixels · P/Esc menu. Gamepad: left stick cursor, A assign, LB/RB skill, right stick scroll, Start menu.
